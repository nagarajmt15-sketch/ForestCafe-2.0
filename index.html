A DIRECTOR'S RETREAT

From Cinema Sets
to Coffee Slopes

  • ✓ Chennai Cinema Foundations: Built my creative career designing cinematic sets for films including Jayam, Something Something, Unakkum Enakkum, Vennila Kabaddi Kuzhu, Sarvam Thaala Mayam, Jeeva, and 300+ commercial ad campaigns.
  • ✓ The Mist of Kalrayan: An impromptu trip to the Kalrayan Hills revealed cool mountain air, raw wilderness, and absolute calm—sparking the decision to leave city studios behind.
  • ✓ 2.25 Acres of Living Earth: With my wife and children by my side, we transformed virgin soil into an organic homestead—cultivating shade-grown coffee, pepper, native fruits, and wild beehives.
  • ✓ The Forest Cafe (2021): Conceived to share our harvests directly—serving freshly roasted estate coffee, authentic Podi Chai, and raw hill honey straight to your table.

“From our farm to your table, every cup and every taste carries a little piece of our journey.”

2021Cafe Opened

For years, my world was framed through camera lenses, production blueprints, and studio backlots across Chennai. Crafting atmosphere for audiences across major films and hundreds of commercials was deeply fulfilling, yet beneath the noise, I longed for an honest, tactile connection to the earth.

When I first visited the Kalrayan Hills, the quiet mountain roads and rolling mist left a profound impression. It wasn't merely a getaway; it felt like home. That encounter inspired my family and me to take a courageous leap—exchanging urban sets for 2.25 acres of untamed slopes.

Together, we cleared the land with reverence for the ecosystem. We nurtured shade-grown coffee, planted silver oak for wind protection, tended pepper vines and banana groves, and introduced bees to help pollinate the forest canopy while providing raw honey.

What began as a quiet family haven grew into something we wanted to share. In 2021, The Forest Cafe opened its doors—not as an ordinary business, but as the living culmination of cinema craft, mountain farming, and hospitality.

Every cup poured here carries the spirit of that transition. Welcome to our table.
